Overview

What is CiteClear?

CiteClear is a citation verification and source-quality tool. It confirms that each citation in your document points to a source that actually exists, is reachable, is current enough for your field, and is quoted or paraphrased accurately. AI-assisted writing produces plausible but fabricated or misquoted citations; CiteClear verifies each source against reality rather than trusting it. The first pass runs entirely in the browser, and deeper AI-assisted review only runs when you explicitly ask for it.

Verification checks

What CiteClear checks

Source existence

Confirms each citation points to a traceable source record or destination — URLs, DOIs, ISBNs, and legal references — before any interpretive review. Deterministic

Broken link detection

Flags malformed, dead, or redirected links so unreachable sources surface before deeper analysis. Deterministic

Citation formatting

Validates format against APA, MLA, Chicago, Bluebook, IEEE, and custom styles, flagging missing fields and ordering problems. Deterministic

Stale source warning

Surfaces publication dates and flags sources older than a configurable threshold — 2, 5, or 10 years depending on your field. Deterministic

Quote/source mismatch

Compares quoted or paraphrased language against the source and flags where wording or meaning appears to drift. AI-assisted

Claim-to-source alignment

Evaluates whether each claim is actually supported by the source it cites, flagging overstatement, drift, and cherry-picking. AI-assisted

Source credibility scoring

Scores source quality from publisher type, authority signals, and context to separate strong evidence from thin citations. AI-assisted

Comparison

CiteClear vs. asking ChatGPT to check

The short answer: a language model that can fabricate a citation is not a reliable verifier of that citation. CiteClear checks sources against reality instead of asking a model to grade its own work.

Asking ChatGPT

Re-runs the same kind of model that can hallucinate sources, can invent confirmations, and gives no deterministic, reproducible audit trail.

CiteClear

Runs deterministic existence, link, format, and staleness checks first, then adds optional AI review for quote fidelity and claim alignment — with a clean, shareable report.

Why it matters

In legal and research writing, one fabricated or misquoted citation can undermine an entire document. Verification, not trust, is the point.

FAQ

Direct answers for source-heavy teams

What is CiteClear?

A citation verification and source-quality tool for legal, academic, research, and AI-assisted writing. It checks whether cited sources are real, relevant, current, and accurately quoted.

Why not just ask ChatGPT to check my citations?

Asking a language model to check citations re-runs the same kind of model that can fabricate them. CiteClear verifies each source against reality instead of trusting a model's word.

Does CiteClear catch hallucinated AI citations?

Yes. It is built to catch fabricated sources, dead links, and quote mismatches that commonly appear in LLM-generated briefs, memos, and reports.

Why does the product lead with local preflight?

Because that is the clearest trust position: check locally first, then only hand off to AI when you explicitly choose that step. Pasted text is not used to train models.

What citation styles are supported?

APA, MLA, Chicago, Bluebook, IEEE, and custom style guides.

Is there a free plan?
