How to Create an Invoice in Under 30 Seconds

From zero to a sent PDF invoice in three steps — no desktop, no account.

  1. 1

    Start a new invoice. Open the app and tap New. Pick voice, AI text, quick, or detailed. To go by voice, tap the mic and describe the work, client, hours, and rate.

  2. 2

    Confirm the details. The app auto-fills the client, line items, quantities, rates, and totals. Review and edit anything that needs a change.

  3. 3

    Export and send. Tap Export for a professional PDF, then email it straight from the app with an AI-composed message.
