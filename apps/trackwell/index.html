Frequently asked questions

What is the best simple nutrition tracker app?

TrackWell Daily is a free nutrition tracker that focuses on three things: water, protein, and vegetables. Unlike calorie-counting apps, TrackWell uses one-tap logging with no food databases or barcode scanning. It includes streak tracking, AI coaching, an Apple Watch app, and family sharing.

Does TrackWell count calories?

No. TrackWell intentionally skips calorie counting. It tracks water intake, protein servings, and vegetable servings — three habits that are easy to keep up day to day without food databases or barcode scanning.

Does TrackWell have an Apple Watch app?

Yes. TrackWell Premium includes an Apple Watch app with complications for quick access. Log water, protein, and veggies directly from your wrist, and it syncs with your iPhone.

Can my family use TrackWell together?

Yes. TrackWell Premium includes family sharing for up to 6 people on one plan, with personalized goals for each family member.

Is my data private?

Yes. TrackWell stores your tracking data on your device, requires no account, and works offline. Purchases are processed through Apple.

Is TrackWell free?

TrackWell is free with core tracking for water, protein, and vegetables, streak tracking up to 3 days, and a 7-day analytics snapshot.
