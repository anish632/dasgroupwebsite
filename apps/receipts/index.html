Features

What Scan Receipts does

Scan Receipts replaces the shoebox of paper receipts and manual expense spreadsheets with a scan-or-speak workflow that auto-categorizes and exports everything your accountant needs — processed entirely on-device.

  1. 1

    Scan any receipt — snap-and-done, zero data entry

    Apple's native document scanner auto-detects the receipt edge and captures it. On-device AI extracts merchant, date, amount, and tax instantly. The receipt is saved automatically — no form, no typing, no review screen required. Scan multiple receipts in a single session with built-in batch mode.

  2. 2

    Voice receipt entry — say the expense, skip the camera

    Say "forty-two fifty at Costco for groceries" and the app creates an expense draft with the amount, merchant, and category already filled in. Review parsed fields before saving. Start voice entry from the app, Siri, or the Shortcuts app.

  3. 3

    11 automatic tax expense categories

    Every receipt is auto-sorted into a tax-friendly category: meals & dining, travel, office supplies, gas & fuel, healthcare, parking, entertainment, utilities, shopping, personal, or other. Categories map to common IRS deduction lines — making Schedule C prep faster.

  4. 4

    Home screen widget — spending at a glance

    See your monthly spending total, receipt count, and top categories right on your home screen without opening the app. Tap the widget to instantly scan a new receipt. Available in small and medium sizes.

  5. 5

    AI spending insights and smart alerts

    On-device AI generates alerts when spending spikes, discovers recurring patterns, identifies savings opportunities, and sends tax deduction reminders. Acts as a personal AI financial analyst powered entirely by your own receipt data — no data sent to a cloud.

  6. 6

    Export to PDF and CSV — tax-ready expense reports

    Generate professional expense reports in PDF or CSV. Send directly to your accountant or import into QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, or any tax software. Each report includes merchant, date, amount, tax, and category for every receipt.

  7. 7

    Business trip tracking with per-category totals

    Create a trip, assign receipts as you go, and automatically total expenses by category. Submit polished expense reports when you return. Designed for travel reimbursements and per diem tracking — no shoebox required.

  8. 8

    Mileage tracking with customizable rates

    Log business miles alongside receipt expenses. Set your own mileage rate to match current IRS standard rates or your employer's reimbursement policy. Mileage appears in exported expense reports alongside your receipt data.

  9. 9

    Find any receipt instantly

    Search by merchant name, amount, date range, or category. Filter by business trip or tax year. Duplicate detection prevents double-counting the same expense. Full receipt history always searchable, always on-device.

  10. 10

    100% on-device — privacy-first, no account required

    All receipt scanning, OCR, and AI processing happens on your iPhone. No cloud uploads, no third-party servers, no data sharing. Your financial data stays on your device. Start scanning immediately — no signup or account creation needed.
