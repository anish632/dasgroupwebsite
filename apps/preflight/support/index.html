Preflight Support

Preflight helps you review a Canva design before publishing. No external account or login is required.

How it works

Open a Canva design, open Preflight, configure any campaign-specific rules, and choose Scan with these rules. Review each finding. Safe fixes are always explicit and only update supported text or solid-color content.

Supported checks

  • Required text and banned phrases
  • CTA phrases and configured brand colors
  • Text size, spacing, URL syntax, and conservative date checks
  • Substantially out-of-bounds supported elements

Only the current Canva page is scanned. Images, multi-page scanning, URL reachability, logo detection, and legal compliance checks are not supported.

Troubleshooting

  • If scanning is unavailable, confirm the app has Canva design content read/write scopes and reopen the app.
  • If a fix fails, rescan the current page; the design may have changed or the target may be locked.
  • Rules are kept in the current app session and are not sent to a server.

Privacy

Preflight reads the current Canva page only to perform requested checks. Processing is local to the app session. See the Das Group Privacy Policy.

Contact support

Email support@dasgroupllc.com with the Canva design type, the rule you were using, and the user-visible error message. Do not send confidential design content unless necessary.
