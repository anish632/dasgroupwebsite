Preflight is a design quality-checking tool provided by Das Group LLC. By using it, you agree to use the app lawfully and to review your designs before publishing.

What Preflight does

Preflight reads the current Canva page to perform the checks you request. Some findings are suggestions and some supported findings can be fixed after your confirmation. The app does not guarantee that a design is error-free or suitable for a particular channel.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for reviewing your design, content, links, dates, brand requirements, and publishing decisions. Preflight is not legal, advertising, regulatory, accessibility, or compliance advice.

Accuracy and availability

Results may include false positives or false negatives because Canva designs and supported APIs vary. The app is provided on an “as available” basis, and functionality may change as Canva updates its platform.
