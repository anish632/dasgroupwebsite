Features

Talk for a minute. Keep a private mood diary.

VoiceJournal replaces typed journaling apps and cloud AI diaries with a local voice workflow: record, transcribe on-device, review mood and topics, then search, streak, and revisit later.

Voice-first journaling

Tap record and talk about your day. Live on-device transcription appears as you speak, and you can edit the text before you save.

On-device mood reflection

Sentiment analysis runs on the device and adds a mood label, score, and topics. It is a mirror for your words, not a diagnosis.

Searchable private diary

Find entries by keyword, mood, or date. Bookmark important moments and open On This Day to replay past reflections.

Daily check-ins and streaks

A daily check-in prompt, a 3-day reflection challenge, and a Home Screen streak widget help the habit stick after the first saved entry.

Siri and Shortcuts

Start a new voice journal with Siri or Shortcuts. Ask for journal stats or today’s mood without opening a typing screen.

Private by design

Journal entries, recordings, and mood history stay on your device. Transcription requires on-device speech recognition. No account is required.

How it works

How to journal by voice on iPhone

Most people finish a first entry in about two minutes. Recording transcription uses Apple’s on-device Speech framework.

  1. Step 1

    Start recording

    Open VoiceJournal and tap record, or start a new voice entry with Siri or Shortcuts.

  2. Step 2

    Speak a reflection

    Talk in your own words. The transcript appears on screen. No internet is required for transcription.

  3. Step 3

    Review mood and topics

    Stop, edit if needed, and see the mood label, sentiment, and topics extracted from what you said.

  4. Step 4

    Save and revisit

    Search later, open On This Day, follow the daily check-in, and watch streaks and mood trends grow.

Frequently asked questions

What is VoiceJournal?

VoiceJournal is a private voice journal for iPhone and iPad from Das Group LLC. You speak a reflection, and the app creates an editable, searchable journal entry with on-device transcription, mood reflection, and topics. It is a reflection tool, not a medical diagnosis.

What is the best private voice journal app for iPhone?

VoiceJournal is built for people who want to journal by talking instead of typing. On-device speech recognition transcribes the entry, on-device sentiment analysis adds mood and topics, and the journal stays on the device. No account is required.

Does VoiceJournal work offline?

Yes. Speech recognition and mood analysis run on the iPhone. You can record, transcribe, and save a journal entry without sending journal content to a cloud AI service.

Does VoiceJournal upload journal entries to the cloud?

No. VoiceJournal does not send journal content to a cloud AI service. Transcription requires on-device speech recognition. Entries, recordings, and mood history are stored locally on the device. Purchases go through Apple. Anonymous product analytics do not include journal content.

How does VoiceJournal mood tracking work?

After you save a spoken entry, VoiceJournal uses on-device sentiment analysis to reflect the tone of the text. It adds a mood label, a sentiment score, and topics so you can review patterns across days and weeks. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional mental-health care.

Can I start a VoiceJournal entry with Siri?

Yes. VoiceJournal includes Siri and Shortcuts support to start a new voice journal entry. You can also check journaling streak and recent mood from Siri, and pin a streak widget to the Home Screen.

Is VoiceJournal free?

VoiceJournal is free to download with a first saved voice entry, today's mood and topics, and a 7-day mood history snapshot. VoiceJournal Pro unlocks unlimited entries, audio playback, full mood history, and weekly emotional patterns. Current US prices are $5.99 monthly, $39.99 yearly, or $79.99 lifetime through the App Store.
