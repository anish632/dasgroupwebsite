Key Features of QuickScan AI

QuickScan combines native computer vision, on-device OCR, machine-learning document classification, and PDF generation into an ultra-fast, privacy-first mobile toolkit.

  1. 1

    3-Second AI Edge Detection & Auto-Capture

    Point your iPhone camera at any paper document, form, or card. QuickScan’s computer vision model detects document boundaries in real time, automatically triggers the capture, and flattens angled perspective distortions in under 3 seconds.

  2. 2

    AI Background Removal & Shadow Elimination

    Messy wooden desks, kitchen counters, or dim lighting? QuickScan isolates the document borders, crops away all background clutter, eliminates harsh hand shadows, and optimizes contrast for a crisp, professional photocopy appearance.

  3. 3

    High-Precision ID Card & Passport Mode

    Scan government driver's licenses, passports, health insurance cards, credit cards, and employee badges with dedicated edge recognition. Produces perfectly scaled, glare-reduced scans ideal for KYC verification and official submissions.

  4. 4

    On-Device OCR Text Extraction (Apple Vision)

    Extract editable, searchable text from any scanned document or photo in milliseconds using Apple's Vision framework. Copy full text to clipboard, search for specific terms, or export searchable PDFs without sending data to third-party APIs.

  5. 5

    10-Type Document Classification & Entity Extraction

    QuickScan uses on-device Natural Language processing to automatically classify scans into Receipts, Invoices, Contracts, IDs, Letters, Forms, Reports, Medical Records, Bank Statements, and Tax Documents. It extracts structured entities like transaction dates, monetary amounts, phone numbers, and names automatically.

  6. 6

    Digital Signatures & Contract Signing

    Sign agreements on the go. Create, save, and reuse handwritten signatures drawn with your finger or Apple Pencil. Place, resize, and rotate signatures anywhere on the document before exporting the finalized PDF.

  7. 7

    Multi-Page Batch Scanning Mode

    Need to digitize a 20-page legal brief or multi-page lease agreement? Rapidly flip through pages while QuickScan auto-captures each sheet and automatically compiles them into an organized, single multi-page PDF document.

  8. 8

    100% Offline & Air-Gapped Privacy Architecture

    All computer vision, OCR inference, and PDF rendering run locally in the secure iOS app sandbox. QuickScan operates completely without Wi-Fi or cellular service, ensuring sensitive legal, financial, and medical documents never touch external servers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about scanning, OCR, signatures, offline privacy, and pricing.

What is the best free document scanner app for iPhone? ↓

QuickScan is a top-rated free AI document scanner app for iPhone (iOS 17+) that scans documents, IDs, receipts, and contracts to clean PDFs in about 3 seconds. It features automatic edge cropping, background removal, on-device OCR text extraction, digital signatures, and 100% offline operation with no account required.

Does QuickScan upload scanned documents to the cloud or remote servers? ↓

No. QuickScan operates on a strict 100% on-device processing model. All computer vision scanning, edge detection, OCR text extraction, document classification, and PDF generation run locally on your iPhone using Apple's native frameworks. No documents, photos, or text ever leave your device.

Can QuickScan scan IDs, driver's licenses, and passports? ↓

Yes. QuickScan includes a dedicated ID scanning mode designed for driver's licenses, passports, health insurance cards, credit cards, and badges. The AI automatically crops the card edges, straightens angles, and eliminates background desk surfaces for a clean scan.

How does on-device OCR work in QuickScan? ↓

QuickScan uses Apple's native Vision framework to perform high-accuracy Optical Character Recognition (OCR) directly on your iPhone. It extracts editable, copyable text from scanned documents, receipts, invoices, and business cards without sending data to an external server.

Can I sign PDF documents and contracts in QuickScan? ↓

Yes. QuickScan includes a built-in digital signature tool. You can draw your signature with your finger or Apple Pencil, save it securely on your device, and place or resize it anywhere on your scanned documents before exporting or sharing.

What document types does QuickScan automatically classify? ↓

QuickScan uses on-device Natural Language processing to classify scans into 10 structured document types: Receipts, Invoices, Contracts, ID Documents, Letters, Forms, Reports, Medical Records, Bank Statements, and Tax Documents. It also automatically extracts structured metadata like dates, currency totals, phone numbers, and addresses.

Does QuickScan work without an internet connection? ↓

Yes. QuickScan works 100% offline. Because all computer vision, OCR, document classification, and PDF compilation execute locally on Apple Silicon, you can scan, edit, sign, and export documents in airplane mode or in remote areas with zero connectivity.

How many free scans are included with QuickScan? ↓

QuickScan is free to download and includes 5 free scans with no account creation or signup required. Free users get AI edge detection, background removal, text clarity enhancement, and PDF export. The Pro upgrade ($4.99/mo or $19.99/yr) adds unlimited scans, watermark-free exports, full OCR text extraction, batch scanning, and signature tools.

Can I automate QuickScan with Siri and Apple Shortcuts? ↓

Yes. QuickScan provides native App Intents and Siri Shortcuts including "Scan with QuickScan", "Voice Scan and Name" (with automatic document type tagging), and "Import to QuickScan", making it fully compatible with Apple Intelligence and iOS workflow automation.
