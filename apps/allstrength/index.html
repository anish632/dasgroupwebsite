Voice Search & Hands-Free Control (VSO)

Never touch your screen with sweaty hands again.

AllStrength AI features an on-device natural speech recognizer built into the active workout screen. As you finish a set of squats or hip thrusts, just speak normally. The app recognizes your voice instantly, logs the set, and initiates your rest timer.

Spoken Audio Coach Feedback

Includes natural audio feedback using Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer. When a rest timer reaches 0 or you ask how many sets remain, the app vocalizes the answer through your iPhone speaker or AirPods without requiring you to glance at your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about AllStrength AI, hands-free voice commands, workouts, and privacy.

What is AllStrength AI?

AllStrength AI is an iPhone strength training app built specifically for women. It includes 30+ guided workouts across calisthenics, glute hypertrophy, hybrid training, and women's health life stages (postpartum recovery, menopause, pelvic floor), accompanied by hands-free voice controls, on-device AI coaching, Lock Screen Live Activities, and 100% private offline storage.

How do hands-free voice controls work during workouts?

During an active workout session, AllStrength AI uses Apple's on-device SFSpeechRecognizer to listen for spoken commands such as "Next set", "Skip rest", "Pause", "Resume", "How many sets left", and "Add 30 seconds". It allows you to advance your workout and control rest clocks without ever touching your iPhone screen with sweaty hands.

Does AllStrength AI include postpartum or menopause workouts?

Yes. The dedicated "Women's Health" category includes routines for postpartum recovery (diastasis recti safe core breathing, pelvic floor activation), prenatal mobility, menopause bone density preservation, and hormone balance flows. All fitness content is informational and designed to complement clinical advice.

Does AllStrength AI require an internet connection or account?

No. AllStrength AI operates on a 100% on-device, local-first architecture powered by Apple's SwiftData. It does not require account creation, logins, or internet access to perform workouts, track streaks, or generate AI coaching insights.

Does AllStrength AI support Live Activities and Dynamic Island?

Yes. Active workout sessions display live on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island via ActivityKit, showing current exercise name, active set counter, and live rest countdown in real time.

Can I use Siri voice shortcuts with AllStrength AI?

Yes. With native App Intents support, you can tell Siri "Start a workout in AllStrength", "My workout stats", "Complete this set", "Pause my workout", or "Log a workout" for seamless hands-free control.

Is my fitness and health data private?

Yes, 100%. All workout histories, exercise logs, and personal records remain strictly on your iPhone in local SwiftData. There are no cloud servers storing your personal data, no ad trackers, and zero telemetry sharing.

Is AllStrength AI free to download?

AllStrength AI is free to download on the App Store with free starter workouts across all 6 categories, standard rest timers, and streak tracking. Optional Premium subscriptions ($6.99/mo, $24.99/6-mo) or a Lifetime one-time unlock ($79.99) provide unlimited access to the complete 30+ workout library and advanced on-device AI coaching.
